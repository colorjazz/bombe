Parent consent letter

A template to send before marking with Burn the Pile. Click the passages highlighted in yellow to replace them with your information, then print, download for Word or copy the text.

  • Ask your principal or school board first. Most school boards decide which AI tools may be used with student work. Parent consent adds to that approval; it does not replace it.
  • Mark by hand the papers of students whose parents decline or do not reply, and keep the signed slips for the whole school year.
  • Keep the “Hide the student’s name before sending” option on, and ask students to write their name only on the “Name:” line.

Re: use of a marking assistance tool

Dear parents,

This year, I would like to use Burn the Pile, a marking assistance tool, for some of your child’s work in [subject]. Before I do, I am asking for your consent.

How it works

  • I take a photo of your child’s paper with my phone or computer.
  • Before anything is sent, your child’s name is hidden directly on my device: the tool does not know whose paper it is.
  • The paper is then read by an artificial intelligence (Gemini, by Google), which suggests errors to correct, a mark and a comment. Google’s servers may be located outside Canada.
  • The paper is then read by an artificial intelligence (Gemini, by Google), in Google’s data centre in Montreal (Quebec, Canada), which suggests errors to correct, a mark and a comment.
  • Burn the Pile does not keep the paper after marking.

What does not change

  • I am the one who assesses your child. I review every suggestion and decide on the final mark.
  • The paper remains your child’s work, and your child keeps ownership of it.
  • This use was approved by [the principal / the school board] on [date].

Your choice

Your consent is free and voluntary. If you decline, I will mark your child’s work the usual way, with no consequence for your child. You can change your mind at any time by letting me know in writing.

Please fill in and return the slip below by [date]. For any question, email me at [email address] or call me at [phone number].

[Your name]
