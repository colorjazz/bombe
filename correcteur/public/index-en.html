Made by a teacher, for teachers.

I teach high-school history in Quebec. I built Burn the Pile to do the first pass of my own stacks of tests, without sending my students’ work just anywhere. I still read every paper: the tool does the first pass, I keep the judgment.

Three steps, a few minutes

No training, nothing to install. It runs in your browser, on a computer or a tablet.

1. Your answer key

Paste your instructions or upload your answer key (PDF, photo). Points per question, accepted answers, criteria: Burn the Pile follows your rubric, or builds a detailed one for you.

2. Student work

Drop the scanned PDF of the whole class, photos or Word documents. Burn the Pile finds where each paper starts, and you can adjust.

3. You approve

A grade and justification for each question, feedback for the student. Adjust a point in one click and download the results for Excel or your markbook.

Check the writing too, right on the paper

A dedicated tab finds the errors on every page, highlights them by category and explains the rule. Perfect for papers you hand back to students.

Frequently asked questions

Does the AI replace my judgment?

No. It suggests a mark and a justification; you remain the marker. Everything can be edited before export, and papers that need a second look are flagged.

Does it work with handwriting?

Yes, with clear scans or photos. If a passage is hard to read, the paper is flagged for review. If the photo is illegible, we never guess a mark: the paper is marked “Illegible” and is not charged.

Can I hide student names?

Yes. Tick “Hide the student’s name before sending”: the top of each paper’s first page is hidden in your browser before sending. A name written elsewhere is not hidden: a student code is safest. You still see the full paper and enter the names yourself.

Which file formats?

Scanned PDFs (one or several papers per file), phone photos (including iPhone photos), Word documents (.docx) and text files. Papers in a single scan are split by student automatically.

Which subjects?

Any subject where an answer key or rubric can judge the answers: history, science, math (answers and work shown), English, languages, short written answers. The more precise your answer key, the more precise the grading.

What is a “page”?

One page of student work (one scanned side or one photo). In quick mode and for language checks, a page counts as 1; in in-depth mode (double grading and reliability), as 2. The answer key is not counted. Failed gradings are not charged.

What happens to my students’ work?

We store no student work, marks or student names. For marking, papers are sent encrypted to Google (Gemini API, paid tier): Google does not use them to train its models and keeps them only briefly, to detect abuse. See our privacy policy.

Do I need an account?

No. You get an access key that holds your page balance. Write it down to use it on another device or to top up.
